# Recipe: problem-solution (12 s)
**Use for:** promote / educate. Pain -> relief -> calm payoff -> CTA. **Workflow:** motion-graphics (<= 10 s) or general-video.
**Worked example:** `projects/twofold-labs/example-problem-solution/` (passes `reel check`; a draft render has been produced).

| Time | Beat | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1-3.5 | **3 slam cuts** naming the pain (<= 4 words each, last one in accent) | camera punch + `slam` on each |
| 3.6-8.0 | **Chaos -> one flow:** scattered nodes drift to a ring, link to an amber hub; payoff line "ONE FLOW." | riser, whoosh, a `tick` per link, `chime` |
| 6.4 | one-line promise (<= 8 words) | fade in, muted/regular |
| 8.3-12 | **CTA card** (name/logo, URL in accent, one-line invite) | `chime`, held 3.7 s |

Copy rules: the pain must be specific to the audience, not generic. Payoff is a short imperative or noun phrase. Never invent numbers ("seven tools" must come from the user/brand).
