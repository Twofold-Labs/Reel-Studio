# Recipe: services-rundown (10 s)
**Use for:** promote / awareness. Hook question -> capabilities pop in one by one -> CTA. **Workflow:** motion-graphics or general-video.

| Time | Beat | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1-2.0 | hook question, 2 lines max, slam | `slam` |
| 2.1-7.0 | 3-6 capabilities: icon (SVG, accent) in a surface circle + a 1-2 word label, one every ~0.5 s | `pop` per item, overshoot ease |
| 7.2-10 | CTA line | `chime` |

Labels come from `brand.yaml: descriptor` / services the user listed. Build icons as inline SVG (simple shapes) or take them from the HyperFrames catalog; never use a third-party logo.
